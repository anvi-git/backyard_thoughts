Backyard is an independent digital magazine and family of specialized publications founded by Antonio Viscusi. Rooted in physics, astronomical research, deep listening, and climate observation, the project is conceived as an intellectual sanctuary: a space where deep-form essays, long-forgotten mathematical anecdotes, and empirical cosmic observations take precedence over hurried digital noise.

The Publication Family

Rather than compressing disparate subjects into a monolithic feed, Backyard is structured around distinct publishing channels, each possessing its own rhythm and investigative lens:

Heralding Time: Longform essays on the history and philosophy of science, physical laws, time measurement, and critical epistemology. From the cycloid's impact on British navigation to the astronomical foundations of ancient calendars.

Last Scattering Surface: A dedicated journal exploring the physics of the cosmos, stellar nucleosynthesis, cosmological expansion, and the background radiation that marks the visible boundary of the observable universe.

Space of Sound: Bi-weekly sound orbits, album explorations, acoustic analyses, and deep listening guides designed for curious ears.

Observe the Fire: Rigorous empirical records, zonal temperature anomalies, and environmental heatmaps tracing our planet's climate crisis from 1880 to the present.

The Blog & The Magazine: Occasional field sketches, philosophical poems, and anthological print-rhythm issues curating the strongest voices from across the Backyard family.

The Editor

Antonio Viscusi holds an MSc in Astrophysics (now at INFN) and a post-graduate master's degree in Space Science. His work bridges theoretical cosmology, astronomical instrumentation, computational modelling, and cultural critique.

“The cosmos is all that is, or ever was, or ever will be. In the backyard of human knowledge, we cultivate questions rather than certainty.”

All publications are distributed openly online and through Substack, preserving full independence and reader-supported scholarship.
